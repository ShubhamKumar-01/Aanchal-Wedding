Frequently Asked Questions ❓

A few helpful details to make your celebration easy and memorable. Tap a question to reveal the answer.

📅 When and where are the celebrations?
Please check the individual event cards above for each ceremony's date, time, and venue. We will update any changes here before the celebration.
👗 What should I wear?
Festive and traditional outfits are welcome. Each event card includes a suggested dress code—for example, yellow or light colours for Haldi and festive party wear for Sangeet. Comfort comes first!
🕒 What time should I arrive?
Please aim to arrive 15–20 minutes before the ceremony's scheduled start so you can settle in and enjoy the celebrations. Check the event details for the exact timing.
🚗 Is parking or transport available?
Parking and transport details will be shared once the venue arrangements are confirmed. Please add the parking location, shuttle details, or local directions to the relevant event's notes.
🍽️ Will food and refreshments be served?
Food and refreshments are planned according to each event's schedule. Please check the event details for meal timings and add any confirmed menu or dietary information here.
📸 Can we take photos and videos?
Of course! We would love you to capture happy memories. During important rituals, please be considerate of the couple, family, and professional photographers so everyone can enjoy the moment.
🎁 Are gifts expected?
Your presence, blessings, and good wishes are the greatest gift. If the couple has a preferred gifting arrangement, add those details here.
☎️ Whom should I contact for help?
For questions about timings, directions, or arrangements, please contact the family wedding coordinator. Add their name and phone number here before sharing the invitation.

Personalize these answers before sharing the invitation. Replace general notes with confirmed venue, parking, contact, and meal information.
